Services

Everything from your yearly checkup to managing a long-term eye condition.

Comprehensive eye exams

A full check of your vision and eye health. Yearly exams can catch diabetes-related changes, vascular problems, and other issues early.

Contact lens fittings

Exams and fittings for glasses and contacts to correct nearsightedness, farsightedness, astigmatism, and presbyopia.

Optical shop

More than 500 frames picked for style, quality, and value, each with a two-year warranty against breakage.

Retinal imaging without dilation

Wide-field Clarus 500 imaging gives a detailed view of the back of your eye, usually without dilating drops.

Eye disease care

Diagnosis and management of cataracts, macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, amblyopia, and more.

Insurance help

Our team explains your benefits and helps you get the most out of your vision and medical coverage.
